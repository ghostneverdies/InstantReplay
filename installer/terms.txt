  - Add a per-user "start with Windows" entry that launches Instant Replay minimized to the system tray. This can be turned off in Settings.

8. DATA AND PRIVACY
Instant Replay records your screen and audio (system audio and, if enabled, your microphone) and keeps the most recent footage in memory. Footage is written to disk only when you save a replay. Instant Replay does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data and contains no network code. All recordings, settings and logs are stored locally on your device. No telemetry or analytics data is gathered. You are responsible for complying with applicable laws and the consent of others when recording.
